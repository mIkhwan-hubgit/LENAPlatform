What LENA is, and what it is not

LENA is

  • A self-help companion for everyday wellbeing
  • Built on validated questionnaires and CBT techniques
  • Developed at IIUM with a consultant psychiatrist
  • Available in English and Bahasa Melayu

LENA is not

  • A diagnosis. Your scores are a starting point for a talk with a professional.
  • An emergency service. If you are in danger, call 999.
  • A source of medication advice or religious rulings
  • A replacement for a counsellor, doctor or psychologist

Your data, in plain words

As a guest

  • Everything you add stays in this browser, on this device only.
  • Clearing your browser data erases it, and nobody can bring it back.
  • Create an account later and it moves into your account.

With a LENA account

  • Your entries are kept safely in your LENA account.
  • Signing out removes them from this device. Sign in on any device to get them back.
  • Delete your account and every entry from Profile, whenever you like.

Chat messages are sent to an AI service so it can reply. LENA has no ads and never sells your data. Read the privacy policy

⏰ Your sleep goal

Most people aged 13 to 18 need 8 to 10 hours of sleep a night, and adults need 7 to 9.

Your last 7 nights

Time in bedYour goal

💡 Habits that help

  • ⏰Wake up at the same time every day, weekends too. A steady wake-up time sets your body clock.
  • 🌙Can't sleep after about 20 minutes? Get up, do something calm in dim light, and go back to bed when you feel sleepy.
  • 💤Keep the bed for sleep. Studying or scrolling in bed teaches your brain to stay alert there.
  • ☀️Get daylight in the morning and dim screens in the hour before bed.
  • ☕Caffeine stays in your body for hours. Try to have none after lunch.
  • 🛌Keep naps short, under 30 minutes, and before late afternoon.
These numbers are rough estimates to help you spot patterns. A bad night now and then is normal. If poor sleep keeps getting in the way of your days, talking to a doctor can help.

About CBT
Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) is one of the most evidence-based psychological treatments in the world. It works by identifying and changing unhelpful thought patterns and behaviours.
The CBT Triangle
Thoughts 💭
↕
Feelings 💗 ↔ Behaviour 🎯
Why there are no worksheets here
Structured CBT exercises such as thought records are built to be reviewed with a clinician, who reads what you wrote and works through it with you. Completed alone, they rarely go deep. LENA therefore offers guided tools you can finish by yourself and feel the benefit of straight away. If you want to work through a thought record properly, do it with a professional.

About LENA

LENA is a mental wellness companion for young people in Malaysia. It helps you notice how you feel, practise skills that help, and find support when you need it.

🧭 What you can do here

📊Track your mood and sleep
📋Take PHQ-9 and DASS-21 screening
💭Practise guided tools
🫁Slow down with breathing
📓Write in a private journal
🎮Play calming and thinking games
🎵Listen to soundscapes
📚Read about mental health
🕌Find faith-based reflections
💬Talk things through with LENA AI

⚠️ What LENA is not

LENA does not diagnose. A screening result is a starting point for a talk with a doctor or counsellor. It is not a diagnosis.

LENA is not an emergency service. If you are in danger, or thinking about ending your life, call a helpline or go to the nearest emergency department now.

LENA AI is a computer program, not a person or a therapist. It can get things wrong. For anything serious, talk to someone you trust or a professional.

🔒 Your data

If you continue as a guest, what you log stays in this browser, on this device. If you sign in to a LENA account, with Google or with your email, it is also backed up to that account, so it is there on any device you sign in on. You can delete it all at any time in Privacy & Data.

There are two exceptions. Messages you send to LENA AI go to an AI service so it can write a reply, and so do the items in a plan when you tap Help me order.

🌿 Who made LENA

LENA was built by a research team at the Kulliyyah of Information and Communication Technology (KICT), International Islamic University Malaysia. A consultant psychiatrist is part of the team.

I'm here to listen and to sit with you through a difficult moment.

I'm not a therapist or a doctor, and I won't tell you what's wrong with you, because that takes a real person who can talk with you properly. But I can stay with you while it's hard. How are you feeling today?
