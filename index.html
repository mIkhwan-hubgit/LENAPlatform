About CBT
Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) is one of the most evidence-based psychological treatments in the world. It works by identifying and changing unhelpful thought patterns and behaviours.
The CBT Triangle
Thoughts 💭
↕
Feelings 😊 ↔ Behaviour 🎯
Why there are no worksheets here
Structured CBT exercises such as thought records are built to be reviewed with a clinician, who reads what you wrote and works through it with you. Completed alone, they rarely go deep. LENA therefore offers guided tools you can finish by yourself and feel the benefit of straight away. If you want to work through a thought record properly, do it with a professional.

Assalamualaikum! I'm LENA 🌿

I'm here to listen and to sit with you through a difficult moment.

I'm not a therapist or a doctor, and I won't tell you what's wrong with you, because that takes a real person who can talk with you properly. But I can stay with you while it's hard. How are you feeling today?
You can talk to me about:
• Anything that's weighing on you
• Exam or family pressure
• Feeling anxious, low, angry or alone

Or I can walk you through a short calming exercise.

If things ever feel unsafe, tell me, and I'll put the right numbers in front of you straight away.
